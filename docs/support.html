Common questions

How do I pay for a job?
Payment is made in the app. Once the tradesperson marks the work complete, a payment button appears on the job. If a deposit was agreed, you pay that first and it comes off the total — it is not an extra charge.
I'm a tradesperson and can't accept jobs.
Customers pay through RockServ, so you need a payment account before you can take work. Open Settings › Payments in the app and follow the steps. It takes a few minutes and needs your bank details and some ID.
When do I get paid?
Your share of each payment goes to your own account automatically, minus RockServ's commission. Settings › Payments, then "View payouts on Stripe" shows exactly when the money reaches your bank.
How do I delete my account?
Open your profile or settings in the app and tap "Delete my account" at the bottom. This is permanent. Completed jobs stay on record without your name attached, as they are the other party's invoice.
I've forgotten my password.
Tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. You'll get an email with a link that opens the app so you can set a new one.
Why can't I share my phone number in the chat?
Contact details are shared automatically once a quote is accepted. Keeping the job in RockServ until then is what protects both sides if something goes wrong.
